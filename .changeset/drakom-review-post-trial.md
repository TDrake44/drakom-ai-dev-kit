`drakom-review post` reads which review threads you resolved: a finding you resolve without a reply or fix is listed as declined, and one you agreed with stays open until a commit fixes it. Re-reviews now carry nits over, keep an open finding's existing thread instead of commenting again, and quote a declined reply in full. A finding that should be fixed before merging is now important, not a nit. As on Copilot's reviews, any open finding means `Changes recommended`, and `Approval recommended` comes only when nothing is open. `Rethink the approach` is no longer a status: after a third round with important findings open, the review says so in its summary. The reviewer's model and effort come from the tool's output rather than the model's own answer, and the reference's base and head command also works on older `gh` versions. Once a review is settled, `post` also reads the pull request's other unresolved threads: it resolves its own and review bots' threads it confirmed fixed, lists people's for them to resolve, and links an existing thread rather than repeating an issue it raises.
